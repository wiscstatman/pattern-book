I realized another simple amendment to Graham/Claude's N+ for 4.  To either of the non-2-count end jugglers, both of whom are on 4-count doubles, put in front of either of them a juggler who exchanges singles on 4-count, in phase with that end juggler.  That forces the former 4-count doubler to be yet another 2-count double/single alternating passer.    It's as if you took original N+, capped the ends with two 4-count-singles passers, and thereby made all four of the originals to be doing the 2-count double/single...
