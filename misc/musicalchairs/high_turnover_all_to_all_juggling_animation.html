Deterministic Right-of-Way Rules

• 1. Obligatory Mover Entry (Right of Way): When a mover approaches station a, if either spot is open they must enter it. If both are open, they take the side opposite their last stint (starting movers: odd → Inner, even → Outer); if only one is open, they must take it.

• 2. Passers Yield & Continue Passing: Stationed jugglers keep passing on Beat 1 until they have completed 2+ passes and the CCW perimeter arc ahead is clear (no mover occupying or advancing into it).

• 3. Advance Only When Blocked: A mover only continues CCW along the circle when both Inner and Outer at station a are occupied.
