# All-to-All Circle Passing: Setup & Movement Rules

## 1. Floor Setup
* **The Moving Circle:** Mark a large circle on the floor (the **perimeter lane**). Everyone who is walking/moving travels **counterclockwise** along this circle.
* **The Passing Stations ($M$ stations):** Place **$M$ radial line segments** evenly around the circle (for example, **$M = 17$ stations** spaced about $6\text{ ft}$ apart along the circle). Each line extends **$3.5\text{ ft}$ inside** the circle and **$3.5\text{ ft}$ outside** the circle, forming two passing spots per station:
  * **Inner Spot:** Inside the circle, facing **outward**.
  * **Outer Spot:** Outside the circle, facing **inward**.
* **Best Group Size for 100% All-to-All:** For $M$ stations, use **$N = 2M - 1$ jugglers** (for example, **$17$ stations and $33$ jugglers**, or **$13$ stations and $25$ jugglers**), choosing a prime number of stations ($13, 17, 19$) so the group never splits into isolated subgroups.

---

## 2. Starting Positions
* **Number Yourselves ($1$ to $N$):** Give each juggler a number from $1$ to $N$.
* **Fill All Spots Except One:**
  * Seat **$M - 2$ pairs** (Inner + Outer facing each other) and **$2$ solo jugglers** (one on an Inner spot, one on an Outer spot) so that **only $1$ passing spot in the entire circle is empty**.
  * Place the **1 remaining juggler** on the moving circle just clockwise of that open spot, facing counterclockwise.
  * *(If starting with more than one mover on the circle, **odd-numbered movers** aim for **Inner** first and **even-numbered movers** aim for **Outer** first whenever both spots at a station are empty.)*

---

## 3. The 6-Beat Rhythm
Everyone keeps a shared **6-beat count** (`1 – 2 – 3 – 4 – 5 – 6`):
* **Beat 1 (PASS):**
  * **Paired jugglers** (Inner and Outer facing each other at the same station) throw a **radial pass** across the circle and count `+1` toward their personal pass total.
  * **Solo jugglers** (with no one across from them) and **Movers** (on the circle) do a **self-throw** in place.
* **Beats 2–6 (MOVE):**
  * All stepping in, stepping out, and walking along the circle happens during beats `2–6`.
  * **Golden Direction Rule:** Every step in the entire pattern goes **forward (counterclockwise)**—never step backward!

---

## 4. Simple Rules for Movers (On the Circle)
As you travel **counterclockwise** along the moving circle, you have **strict right-of-way** over anyone trying to exit a passing spot:

1. **Must Enter an Open Spot:** When you arrive at the station immediately ahead of you, if **either** the Inner or Outer spot is open, you **must step in** (you cannot skip an open station).
2. **Which Side to Pick:**
   * If **only one spot** (Inner *or* Outer) is open, take that open spot.
   * If **both spots** at that station are open, take the **opposite ring** from the one you passed in last time (if you haven't passed yet: **odd numbers take Inner**, **even numbers take Outer**).
3. **Keep Moving Only When Blocked:** Only if **both** the Inner and Outer spots at the station ahead are already occupied do you stay on the circle and advance counterclockwise to the next station.

---

## 5. Simple Rules for Passers (In an Inner or Outer Spot)
Once you step into a passing spot, you only need to track **how many passes you have made in that spot** (minimum **$2$ passes**):

1. **Stay Until You Complete 2+ Passes:** You cannot leave your spot until you have exchanged at least **2 radial passes** with a partner across from you. (If your opposite spot is empty, wait patiently and do self-throws until a mover steps in to pair with you.)
2. **Yield to Circle Traffic:** Once you have **2 or more passes**, look at the moving circle immediately ahead of you (counterclockwise):
   * **If a mover is on or stepping into that section of the circle:** Stay in your spot and **keep passing** on Beat 1—movers on the circle always have right-of-way!
   * **If the circle ahead is completely clear:** Step **forward (counterclockwise)** onto the moving circle during Beats `2–6`, and remember to aim for the **opposite side** (`Inner ↔ Outer`) next time you have a choice.
3. **One Exit at a Time:** If both you and your partner reach `2+` passes at the same time and the circle ahead is clear, take turns exiting (Inner exits on even cycles, Outer exits on odd cycles—or simply alternate who steps out first at your station).

---

## Quick Pocket Summary for Participants
> **"Walk counterclockwise; movers have right-of-way. If a station ahead has an open spot, you must step in (pick the opposite side from your last stint if both are open). Once in a spot, make at least 2 passes—then step forward onto the circle as soon as the lane ahead is clear!"**
