Sliding-Vacancy Bubble Rule

• Setup: N = 2M − 1 jugglers occupy M − 1 paired segments plus 1 mover on the perimeter just clockwise of empty segment k.

• Beat 1 (Pass): All M − 1 facing pairs pass radially across their segments.

• Beats 2–6 (Bubble Shift): Vacancy shifts CCW (k → k−1 mod M) with alternating inner/outer parity so every juggler visits both rings and meets all N − 1 partners.
