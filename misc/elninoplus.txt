The pattern book has a different half-way built pattern called El Niño Plus by Graham, but I don't think that version quite captures the je ne sais quoi of the original pattern, and it's also different from the version we tried on Sunday.

The 4 feeding passes are all crossing double passes in 1-count. All feedee passes are straight singles. Note that the feeder throws 6 passes in a row: straight single, the 4 doubles in the feed, and then a final single to switch the feeder. The beat where the feeders switch is actually a triangle pass... It's 9 passes out of 16 beats for all 4 jugglers.

It's a tough pattern, but I think we could run it... Sunday?
